# Story Bank — Master STAR+R Stories

This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Each evaluation (Block F) adds new stories here. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file — your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" → combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
   - "Tell me about your most impactful project" → pick your highest-impact story
   - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" → find a story with a Reflection

### [Leadership + Delivery] Enterprise AI Platform From Strategy to Execution
**Source:** Decos - Enterprise AI Platform (Dec 2025-Present)
**S (Situation):** The organization needed an enterprise AI platform with multiple capability tracks (multi-agent workflows, integrations, document and knowledge processing, memory, voice) while keeping architecture and delivery aligned.
**T (Task):** Own the product roadmap and technical alignment so strategy translated into implementable increments across teams.
**A (Action):** Defined roadmap priorities, aligned architecture across domains, and drove cross-system execution using LangGraph, MCP connectivity, and retrieval-centric integration patterns while contributing hands-on during implementation cycles.
**R (Result):** Created a unified direction across business, architecture, and engineering, reducing fragmentation risk and enabling steady delivery of platform capabilities.
**Reflection:** Product leadership is strongest when paired with implementation fluency; it increases team trust and speeds decisions.
**Best for questions about:** Leading ambiguity, cross-functional alignment, strategic thinking, technical product ownership.

### [Product + AI] Multilingual Municipal Chatbot With Policy Logic
**Source:** Decos - Student Transport Chatbot (Oct 2025-Feb 2026)
**S (Situation):** Parents faced a complex, policy-sensitive application flow for student transport, varying by municipality and language needs.
**T (Task):** Deliver a reliable conversational product that improved user completion while respecting municipal policy differences.
**A (Action):** Led roadmap and delivery, designed multilingual flows (Dutch, English, Turkish, Arabic), implemented dynamic field collection and municipality-specific eligibility logic, integrated external search/distance/cost services, and reinforced reliability with memory-aware orchestration and validation.
**R (Result):** Delivered a production-ready guided application experience that balanced accessibility, policy compliance, and operational robustness.
**Reflection:** In public-sector AI products, quality comes from integrating policy, UX, and system reliability from day one.
**Best for questions about:** Customer-centric design, AI product delivery, stakeholder management, quality in production.

### [Platform Evolution] Turning Reporting Into a Strategic Product Layer
**Source:** Decos - JOIN-DCP evolution (ongoing)
**S (Situation):** JOIN-DCP had strong core case-management capabilities, but customers needed better cross-service reporting, sharing, and analytical access.
**T (Task):** Expand product value through a reporting and analytics layer without destabilizing core platform priorities.
**A (Action):** Led product direction for Query Builder, subscriptions, sharing, API access, and AI-layer exploration; guided federation and semantic architecture decisions (Trino and Cube.js patterns); translated tender/demo requirements into scoped roadmap increments; coordinated UX and developer tooling improvements.
**R (Result):** Advanced JOIN-DCP toward a broader AI-augmented work platform while preserving core operational value for public-sector customers.
**Reflection:** Platform evolution succeeds when customer demand, architecture decisions, and delivery trade-offs are explicitly connected.
**Best for questions about:** Product strategy, stakeholder trade-offs, architectural collaboration, platform thinking.
